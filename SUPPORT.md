# Kittehnaut support

_Kittehnaut: Space Kitty Rider. The same page is at https://kittehnaut.app/support.html._

Something not working, an idea for a new item, or a level you're proud of? Get in touch: **komponentapps@protonmail.com**. It helps to say which phone or tablet you're playing on.

## Quick answers

**How do I share a level?** In Freerider, tap the share button on a saved level, or use the share button while building one. Your friend opens it with "Open a shared level".

**Where do fish come from?** Riding new stretches of line, going through rings, catching stars, collecting yarn, pulling off tricks and clearing a level for the first time, plus a daily bonus. You can also buy packs.

**I'm stuck on a level.** Press Skip on the level bar and watch a short ad to open the next one. You can come back and clear it any time.

**Why is an item locked?** Some items have to be unlocked for Freerider, with fish or by watching short ads. Inside a level, the items it gives you are always free.

**I bought something but didn't get it.** Open the game again while online: an unfinished purchase is delivered when the game next starts. Bundles and Remove ads can also be brought back with Restore purchases in the store. If it still doesn't arrive, email us with the date of the purchase.

**How do I turn off the ads between rides?** Buy Remove ads in the store. It's a one-off purchase.

**Can I move my cats and levels to a new phone?** Not yet: everything is kept on the device. Share your levels as codes to keep a copy.

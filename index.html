Kittehnaut

Space Kitty Rider

Draw a path through space and send your cat sliding, flipping and warping along it. Build loops, jumps and switchbacks, then throw in boosts, bounce pads, portals, cannons, pinball flippers, path switches, gear trains and black holes, and watch your cat ride the lot.

Dress your cat for the trip with helmets, hats, moustaches, capes and whole-cat themes from zombie to galaxy. Save your levels, replay your best runs in slow motion, and send a level to a friend as a code they can open in their own game.

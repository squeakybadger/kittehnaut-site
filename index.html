How it goes

Draw, then Ride

One finger draws a line through space. Press Ride and your cat slides along it. Two fingers move and zoom.

Boosts and bounces

Boosts, bounce pads, trampolines, rings of fire, yarn, stars, fireworks, portals and low-gravity zones.

Big rides

Cannons, gear trains, loop-the-loops, pinball flippers, path switches, fiery gates, a UFO's tractor beam and a black hole.

Watch it back

Every ride is a replay. Slow it down or rewind to see exactly how that jump went.

Kitteh Designer

Design and name your cats. Helmets and hats, glasses, moustaches, capes, sweaters, tutus and whole-cat themes.

Fish and the store

Rides earn fish, and there's a daily bonus. Spend them on new things for your cat.

Save and share

Keep your levels, and send one to a friend as a level code they can open in their own game.

Achievements

Something to aim for, through Google Play Games or Game Center.

For phones and tablets, no account needed. Free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases, including a one-off Remove ads.

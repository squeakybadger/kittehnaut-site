Kittehnaut: Space Kitty Rider

Something not working, an idea for a new item, or a level you're proud of? Get in touch: komponentapps@protonmail.com. It helps to say which phone or tablet you're playing on.

Quick answers

How do I share a level? On the Levels screen, tap the share button on a level, or use the share button while building one. Your friend opens it with “Open a shared level”.

Where do fish come from? Riding new stretches of line, going through rings, catching stars, collecting yarn and pulling off tricks, plus a daily bonus. You can also buy packs.

I bought something but didn't get it. Open the game again while online: an unfinished purchase is delivered when the game next starts. Bundles and Remove ads can also be brought back with Restore purchases in the store. If it still doesn't arrive, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I turn off the ads between rides? Buy Remove ads in the store. It's a one-off purchase.

Can I move my cats and levels to a new phone? Not yet — everything is kept on the device. Share your levels as codes to keep a copy.
